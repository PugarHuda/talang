01 · Lifecycle

How a repo lives

Every step is a Daml choice the contract re-checks. Nothing settles on trust.

  1. 01

    Request

    Borrower

    Only the invited lenders see it, and it carries no rate.

  2. 02

    Sealed quotes

    Each lender

    Full principal locked behind the quote. Rivals and the regulator never receive it.

  3. 03

    Award

    Borrower

    Atomic opening leg: collateral pledged, cash delivered, losers refunded unrevealed.

  4. 04

    Margin · Substitution · Roll

    Lender and borrower

    Calls priced from a fresh mark and refused while covered. Swaps and rolls re-check coverage.

  5. 05

    Repurchase or Default

    Borrower · Lender

    Principal plus ACT/360 interest home, fee to the venue. An unanswered call or past maturity hands the pledge to the lender.

04 · BitSafe Decentralization Manager

Marks no single party can move

Margin is computed from a mark that k of n independent pricers must confirm. A lender can be a syndicate that quotes, calls margin, defaults and offers rolls only after a threshold of its members confirms.

  • Uses BitSafe's released governance-action-v1 and governance-core-v1 DARs unchanged.
  • Threshold refusal and execution proven in Daml scripts and on a Canton participant.
  • Run on a three-participant DecMan LocalNet: it still publishes with one node down, not with two.

05 · Canton Token Standard

Real assets on both legs

Cash such as USDCx and collateral such as CBTC as CIP-0056 allocations. Repurchase unwinds them, default executes them, margin and substitution add or swap them.

  • Repurchase appears in any CIP-0056 wallet as a standard allocation request.
  • Built and tested on the Allocation / AllocationRequest interfaces, against a registry implementing them.
  • CBTC and Canton Coin run through their live registries on DevNet.
  • USDCx not yet: DevNet has no USDCx registry.
